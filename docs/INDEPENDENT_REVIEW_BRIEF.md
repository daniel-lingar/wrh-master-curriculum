## 3. Highest-risk unresolved passages (BLOCKING — Session 20)

These four require independent clinical judgment before any delivery decision. Full detail: register entries WRH-ER-001–004; proposals PCC-001–004.

1. **ER-001** (Session 20, line 65): Suicidal ideation presented as a "somatic marker" of a healing law. **Conflicts** with SPRC safe-messaging guidance ("Don't normalize suicide… suicidal ideation is not normal") and with the VA/DoD CPG, which treats ideation as a warning sign/risk domain. *Nothing in the reviewed literature frames SI as a healing milestone.*
2. **ER-002** (Session 20, line 49): Acute crisis called a "biological necessity." No physiological support located; combined with ER-001, implies suicidal crisis is biologically necessary.
3. **ER-003** (Session 20, line 67): Deterministic "rebirth" promise to participants in crisis: "the darkness is temporary; on the other side is rebirth." Help-seeking/hope is supported (Papageno effect); a guaranteed outcome is not.
4. **ER-004** (Session 20, line 12): Session teaches that deterioration ("dark night of the soul") is a *necessary* healing phase. The concept is 16th-century Carmelite mysticism, not clinical science.

## 4. Other safety-significant findings

- **ER-013** (Session 01, line 45): Threat system described as "**permanently** soldered to ON" — contradicted by extinction/treatment-neuroimaging literature AND by the curriculum's own "recalibration" language. Risks teaching hopelessness.
- **ER-011 / ER-012** (Crisis_Package.md): The WRH "RGP" protocol is unvalidated (zero validation evidence located) yet presented as an "immediate intervention"; the 5-step handoff is unvalidated, and its trigger ("inability to self-regulate using RGP") places clinical judgment on non-clinical facilitators. The SPI evidence behind the handoff is real but narrower than WRH implies (non-randomized; behavior not ideation; adults only).
- **ER-007** (Session 76): The "most attempts within 10 minutes" statistic is overstated (actual: 44.1% ≤10 min, median 20 min — Paashaus et al. 2021, full text). The means-restriction teaching built on it is separately well-supported.
- **ER-009** (Session 76): The moral-injury graft misattributes a WRH synthesis to Joiner's theory.
- **ER-006 / ER-010 / ER-015**: Polyvagal-theory framing presented as settled physiology in two sessions without attribution or acknowledgment that the theory is scientifically contested (Grossman et al. 2023; Roelofs 2017).

## 5. Inspected evidence and its limits

- **Overall result:** 0 of 15 claims are supported exactly as written. 3 conflict with the evidence (ER-001, ER-013, ER-009 graft). 9 support only a narrower statement. 6 describe theory/metaphor rather than established mechanism.
- **Full-text inspection:** SPRC guidance; VA/DoD CPG v3.0 pocket card; Tedeschi & Calhoun 2004; Kircanski et al. 2012; Paashaus et al. 2021; Ribeiro et al. 2016; Stanley et al. 2018; Zaccaro et al. 2018; Zhu et al. 2018; Manthey et al. 2021; Porges 2004 (partial).
- **Abstract-only / secondary-only:** Lieberman 2007; Torre & Lieberman 2018; Niederkrotenthaler 2010; Helgeson et al. 2006; Dąbrowski 1970; Simon 2001; Deisenhammer 2009; Franklin 2017; Van Orden 2010; Chu 2017; Ma 2016; Litz 2009; Grossman 2023; Grossman & Taylor 2007; Roelofs 2017; Stanley & Brown 2012; Balban 2023; Lehrer 2020; Nuij 2021; Mann 1999. **Abstract-only findings carry more uncertainty; the reviewer should inspect full texts where a disposition turns on them.**
- **Known citation-chain hazard:** Paashaus et al. 2021's introduction misattributes a "74.4% within 10 min" figure to Deisenhammer et al. — do not rely on secondhand statistics anywhere in this literature.
- **What "not supported" means here:** "Support not located in this search" — not proof that no evidence exists. Specialists may surface adjacent work this phase did not.
