### Open items recorded across topics (not resolved by alignment)

These are the recorded open items from the two extraction files. None of them was resolved by editing participant-facing text in Phase 5, and none is closed by this manifest.

1. **Guide vs. workbook self-assessment format difference (claims-05-08 §5.5).** The facilitator guides score Recognize / Own / Change with a behavioural descriptor in every cell (scores 0–2); the workbooks score the same three skills with 0 — Not Yet / 1 — Sometimes-Afterward / 2 — I Can Practice It checkboxes and a single skill statement per row. Skill correspondence is exact; the formats differ. The new Workbook Lessons 05–08 follows the Batch 1 workbook precedent (as the governing workbook shell requires) and draws its row statements from the guide's score-2 descriptors. Harmonising the two formats themselves is an owner decision; no file was changed for this.
2. **Explain/Separate ordering difference (claims-05-08 §7, cross-topic).** The lessons and workbooks order the sequence … Explain → Separate …; the Facilitator Guide Lessons 05–08's completion architecture (Guide lines 644–658) summarises it as Recognize → Separate → Explain → Protect boundaries → Identify responsibility → Restore agency → Practice → Carry forward. No step is added, dropped, or redefined by the guide summary. It was not changed: reordering a governing summary exceeds a minimal alignment pass. Recorded so a reviewer can disposition it.
3. **Workbook carry-line clarification (claims-01-04 finding A-07).** Each workbook lesson has exactly one Carry-It Statement; each lesson file has eight carry lines (Carry It + Carry-Forward × 4 tracks), all different, and Lessons 2–4's workbook statements match no track line exactly. Phase 5 clarified rather than harmonised: a back-matter paragraph was added to Workbook Lessons 01–04 (register WRH-D-ER-055) stating that the workbook statement is the shared cross-track statement and the track lines remain in the lesson files and are not replaced by it; the new Lessons 1–4 guide prints the track lines and the workbook statement side by side, labelled. For Lessons 5–8 the workbook's single Carry-It per lesson was taken from the guide's practicum line rather than by choosing one track's line over the others (claims-05-08 §5.6). Per-track Carry It / Carry-Forward variation inside the lessons is the lessons' own consistent structure in all 16 track sections of Lessons 5–8 (and likewise in Lessons 1–4) and was preserved. Which line, if any, is canonical remains unstated by design of the clarification, not by oversight.
4. **The governing sequence is stated three ways (claims-01-04 §2.3).** Core-curriculum README: "Show it → Name it → Explain it → Separate it → Interrupt it → Practice it → Carry it" (7 steps, with "Show it"); lesson files: "Seven-Step Application" (Recognize first, no "Show it"); workbook back matter: a 10-item sequence ending "…→ Carry It". ## What verification does and does not establish

**What was done in Phase 5, and what it establishes:**

- **Structural alignment checks.** For each topic, the lesson, workbook, and facilitator guide were read against each other for definitions, examples, exercises, terminology, numbering, step order, and intended takeaway; disagreements found were either fixed with the minimal edits recorded in the claim register (A-01–A-08, R1–R4, plus the authorship of the two missing companions, whose content was drawn from the corresponding lessons and guide rather than invented) or recorded above as open items. This establishes that, at the level of those checks, the twelve files correspond as described in the alignment table — no more.
- **Claim extraction.** 126 material claims were extracted verbatim with locations and entered in the claim register under the seven-field schema in `docs/EVIDENCE_BASE.md`. This establishes a traceable inventory of what the package asserts. Extraction is not evaluation: inclusion in the register is not support for a claim.

**What it does not establish:**

- **Structural alignment ≠ scientific verification.** That a definition, exercise, or takeaway is stated consistently in three files says nothing about whether it is true, effective, or safe. Of the 126 claims, only 5 have any inspected evidence at all, and only via Phase 4 dossier investigation of a claim *family* (T3 breathing, T6 freeze≠refusal, T7 permanence) in master-session material — in 3 of those 5 the exact Lane D wording remains uninvestigated–unresolved, and the other 2 are conforming at family level only. The remaining 121 claims have no inspected evidence, and all 71 claims for D-05–D-08 are explicitly uninvestigated–unresolved. No Lane D claim in this package may be described as evidence-based, evidence-informed, or clinically validated on the basis of this phase.
- **Alignment ≠ qualified review.** Every register entry's reviewer field is PENDING: no clinician, legal/privacy reviewer, accessibility reviewer, or site-implementation reviewer has reviewed any claim, lesson, or procedure in this package, and no required-edit decision has been recorded for any entry. The flagged items — above all the facilitator guides' de-escalation sections and the Lessons 1, 3, and 4 distress/escalation dependencies — remain unmet dependencies for delivery, not matters this phase disposed of.
- **A development package ≠ delivery authority.** This manifest and the claim register are development records on a review branch. Delivery remains withheld, 0 of 6 release gates are satisfied, no setting or site (Corrections, Reentry, Veteran, Workplace) is approved, and nothing in Phase 5 changes that status.
