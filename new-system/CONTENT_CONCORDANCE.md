which master session and which participant handout, based on *content actually read*
(title + body + mechanism + tool), not file numbers.

`session-14.md`) and all 13 handouts (`new-system/handouts/handout-01.md` through

session they belong to (see Mismatch Resolutions below). Pair by content, not by number.

## Mismatch Resolutions

### Why the numbers don't line up (verified)

In the **master** curriculum, Session 10 was dissociation and Session 13 was
noncompliance-misclassification. The rewrite **inserted a new session** —
"Physiological Stress Response and Bodily Signaling" (the body-signaling session) —
**as Session 10**, shifting dissociation to 13 and noncompliance-misclassification to 14
(the rewrite's Session 11, institutional misreading, kept its master number).
The handouts were written against the **master numbering** and were never renumbered.

Result:

- `handout-10` ("Leaving Your Body Was the Only Exit Left") is about **dissociation**
  → correctly pairs with rewritten **Session 13**, not Session 10.
- `handout-13` ("They Called It Noncompliance Because They Could Not See Freeze") is
  about **freeze mislabeled as noncompliance**
  → correctly pairs with rewritten **Session 14**, not Session 13.
- `handout-11` and `handout-12` happen to align with Sessions 11 and 12 by both
  number and content; `handout-09` aligns with Session 9. The offset is **not
  constant**, so no blanket renumbering was applied.
- There is **no handout-14** and none was invented. Session 10 has no handout at all —
  this is a **confirmed gap**, not an unresolved pairing.

### Cross-reference corrections made (Phase 1)

- `sessions/session-09.md` "Cliffhanger into Session 10" previously named Session 10
  as *Dissociative Protective Disengagement States* ("Leaving Your Body Was the Only
  Exit Left") — a leftover from the master sequence. It now names the actual Session 10:
  *Physiological Stress Response and Bodily Signaling* ("The Body Moves Before the
  Mind Knows Why"). The related Implementation-Notes bullet ("Session 10 moves into
  dissociation…") was corrected to describe the actual Session 10 topic.
- No session file references a handout by number, so no "see handout N" references
  needed fixing. Session Implementation Notes reference *tool names* (e.g. "Freeze
  First Aid"), which match the paired handouts' tools.

### Known gap — Session 15 teaser

Session 14's cliffhanger teases Session 15 ("Nervous System Hijack and Survival State
Dominance — Your Nervous System Took the Wheel Before You Could Choose"). That session
does not exist in `new-system/` and was not invented in this phase. Its topic matches
**master** Session 15, *Autonomic Nervous System Dysregulation Patterns*
("Your Nervous System Has Been Hijacked" — content read and confirmed, about
sympathetic dominance), should the rewrite lane extend.

### Master sessions with no rewrite counterpart

- Master **Session_14** — *Dissociation as Adaptive Protective Function* (a second
  dissociation session in the master) — has no counterpart in the rewrite set.
- Master Sessions **15–26** are outside the 14-session rewrite scope.
- Master Session_12 has a same-name file plus a separate `Session_12_Pilot_Portal.md`;
  the pairing is to *Internal Behavioral Conflict Systems*.
